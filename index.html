About Us

A Legacy of Exquisite
Craftsmanship

At Aabhushan Sales, we craft and supply a wide range of micro imitation jewellery. Based in Rajkot, Gujarat, our workshop blends traditional artistry with modern precision engineering.

Every piece we create undergoes careful quality checks — from raw material selection to the final polish. We serve retailers and wholesalers, delivering jewellery that looks luxurious without compromise.
