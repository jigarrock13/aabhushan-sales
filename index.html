About Us

A Trusted Name in Imitation Jewellery

At Aabhushan Sales, we have been shaping the world of micro imitation jewellery for over two decades. Based in Surat — the jewellery capital of India — our workshop blends traditional artistry with modern precision so every piece looks luxurious without the luxury price.

From raw-material selection to the final micro polish, each design passes rigorous quality checks. We supply retailers, boutiques, wholesalers, and exporters across India and beyond — jewellery they can resell with confidence.
